Consent to the processing of personal data

Version of: 04.11.2026

1. Who gives consent and to whom

I, the data subject, acting freely, of my own will and in my own interest, give my consent to the operator — the administrator of the Internationals website (internationals.site) (the “Operator”) — to process my personal data on the terms below, in accordance with Article 9 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data”. Operator contact: mailtomorrow@yandex.ru.

2. What data I provide

  • name, email address, telephone number or messenger name;
  • the content of my inquiry, and the documents and texts I send for translation;
  • the text of a review and a voice recording, if I add them;
  • technical visit data (IP address, browser, device, cookies), if I accepted cookies in the banner on the Site.

I do not give the Operator special categories of personal data, unless they are contained in a document I send for translation myself: in that case they are used only to fulfil my order.

3. For what purposes

  • receiving and handling my inquiry, calculating the price, performing the translation and contacting me about the order;
  • receiving a review and replying to it;
  • operation of the Site and visit statistics (only if I accepted cookies);
  • compliance with the law (accounting and tax records).

4. What is done with the data

Collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification, use, deletion and destruction, with and without automated means. The data is stored in the Russian Federation in the services of Yandex.Cloud LLC and Yandex LLC (hosting, email, web analytics). The Operator does not disclose my data to other third parties except as provided by law and does not carry out cross-border transfer.

5. Term of consent and withdrawal

The consent is valid until the purposes of processing and the retention periods stated in the Personal Data Processing Policy are reached, or until it is withdrawn. I may withdraw consent at any time by writing to mailtomorrow@yandex.ru. After withdrawal the Operator stops processing and deletes the data within the period set by law, except information it is required to keep by law.

6. Separate consent to publish a review

If I send a review through the review form and tick the second box, I separately allow the Operator to publish my name, the review text and my voice recording on the reviews page of the Site, which is open to an unlimited number of people. This consent is valid until withdrawn; it can be withdrawn separately from the consent to processing by writing to mailtomorrow@yandex.ru, after which the review will be unpublished.

7. How consent is given

Consent is given by ticking the box next to the form on the Site and submitting the form. By ticking it I confirm that I have read the Personal Data Processing Policy and understand my rights stated in it.
