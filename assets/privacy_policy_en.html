Personal Data Processing Policy

Effective date and last update: 04.11.2026

1. General provisions

This Policy is drawn up in accordance with Federal Law No. 152-FZ of 27 July 2006 “On Personal Data” and defines what personal data is processed, for what purpose, for how long and where, when you use the website internationals.site (the “Site”), including its language sections. The Site is the Russian-language version of the Internationals project, adapted for users from Russia and the CIS.

2. Operator

The personal data operator is the administrator of the Internationals website: the person who manages the Site, its request forms and the mailbox mailtomorrow@yandex.ru, and who determines what data is collected through the Site and why. Details about the operator are contained in the register of personal data operators kept by Roskomnadzor.

The telephone numbers and messengers shown on the Site belong to translators whom you contact directly. When you call or write to such a number, you communicate with that translator personally, not through the Site: the Operator does not receive the content of that communication and does not pass to translators the data you leave on the Site. No third-party specialists are engaged to process data from the Site.

Email for requests and inquiries from data subjects: mailtomorrow@yandex.ru.

4. Where data is stored

Personal data that you provide through the Site is recorded and stored in databases and storage located in the Russian Federation, in the services of Yandex.Cloud LLC and Yandex (email). We do not carry out cross-border transfer of personal data.

If you write to us via a messenger (WhatsApp, Telegram), you use the service of its owner and accept its terms. We recommend email for sending documents.

5. Retention periods

  • documents and texts sent for translation — deleted within 30 days after the order is completed;
  • correspondence and order details (name, contact, content of the request) — kept for 3 years after the order is completed, then deleted;
  • inquiries that did not lead to an order — kept for up to 1 year;
  • reviews published with your consent — until you withdraw consent to publication;
  • technical visit data — no longer than 12 months;
  • information that must be kept by law (accounting and tax records) — for the periods set by law.

When the purposes of processing are achieved, the retention periods expire or consent is withdrawn, the data is deleted or anonymised.

6. Disclosure to third parties

The Operator does not sell personal data and does not disclose it to third parties, except to:

  • Yandex.Cloud LLC and Yandex LLC — hosting of the Site, data storage, email and web analytics;
  • state authorities — only in the cases and manner provided by law.

The name, review text and voice recording submitted through the review form are displayed publicly on the reviews page only with your separate consent to publication.

7. Cookies and analytics

The Site uses cookies to operate and the Yandex Metrica service (Yandex JSC, Russia) for visit statistics. Metrica is loaded only after you click “Accept” in the cookie banner; if you click “Decline” or do not respond, analytics is not enabled. Session recording (Webvisor) is switched off. Foreign analytics services are not used on the Site. You can withdraw consent by clearing the site data in your browser: the banner will appear again. If you disable cookies in your browser settings, some functions of the Site may not work correctly.

8. Your rights

  • to obtain information about which of your data we process, on what basis and to whom it was disclosed;
  • to demand clarification, blocking or deletion of data if it is incomplete, outdated or processed unlawfully;
  • to withdraw consent to processing, and separately to withdraw consent to the publication of a review;
  • to appeal against the Operator’s actions to Roskomnadzor or in court.

Write to mailtomorrow@yandex.ru. We reply to a request about your data no later than 10 business days after receiving it.

9. How we protect data

The Site works over the secure HTTPS protocol. Only the Operator has access to the data. Accounts are protected by passwords and two-factor authentication; devices use antivirus protection and current updates. In the event of a data leak we will notify Roskomnadzor within the time limits set by law.

10. Changes to the Policy

We may update the Policy. The current version is always published on this page with the date of the update. If the changes concern the purposes of processing, we will ask for your consent again.

Consent to the processing of personal data is given separately: see Consent to the processing of personal data (in Russian). This Policy is for information only and does not replace the consent.
